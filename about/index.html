About us

Strata offshoring, built by people who know strata

Strata Staff Global is a premium offshore capacity solutions provider serving strata agencies across Australia and Canada. We connect strata businesses with strata-trained teams who are ready to work from day one.

Humble beginnings

Three computers and a water dispenser

Owners and co-founders Trevor McGuinness and Paul Miller wanted to test a business idea in the Philippines. After several trips and a lot of deliberation, they started beta testing strata administration work offshore, and Strata Staff launched in April 2019 in a small office in Mabalacat, Pampanga.

The team started with three computers and a water dispenser, and spent countless hours documenting every strata admin task while servicing a few agencies on casual hours. Dan Fabros joined in late 2020 to help with operations and scaling. The business kept going through earthquakes, lockdowns and the COVID-19 pandemic.

Today Strata Staff handles more than 200,000 individual strata tasks a month, from archiving and levy queries to S184/S22 certificates, financial statements, inbox management and AGM agendas.
