## Users

Two audiences, in order of who the surface must convert:

1. **Strata / property management agency principals and operations leaders in Australia and Canada** who are carrying a staffing cost problem. They are usually the owner, CEO, or head of operations of a firm managing anywhere from a few hundred to several thousand lots. They are evaluating offshore capacity providers, often for the first time, and they are risk-averse: they have tried (or heard about) generalist BPOs whose staff did not understand strata, legislation, or their platform.
2. **Philippines-based strata professionals (accountants, administrators, EAs, compliance and insurance specialists, customer care)** evaluating Strata Staff as an employer, and applicants in the live hiring pipeline.

## Product Purpose

Strata Staff Global places and manages purpose-trained offshore specialists — accountants, administrative specialists, executive assistants, compliance and insurance specialists, and customer care staff — inside strata and property management agencies in Australia and Canada. It exists because agency growth is capped by onshore salary cost. Success is a booked discovery call from a qualified agency decision-maker, and secondarily a completed job application.

## Positioning

Strata Staff is not a generalist BPO. Every placement is trained specifically in strata operations, is familiar with Australian and Canadian strata legislation, and arrives already fluent in the platforms agencies actually run (StrataMax, Urbanise, Stratafy, MRI, Strata Vault, PIQ, Smata and others — 13+ named). The mechanism is a documented task library plus the in-house Foundation Training Program and Strata Staff Academy: operations are documented task-by-task and staff are trained against them before endorsement to an agency, which is why ramp-up is short and output is auditable. A generalist offshore staffing firm cannot truthfully copy the strata-specific training and legislation fluency.

## Operating Context

- Agencies evaluate by talking to people: the primary conversion is a **free 30-minute discovery call**, then a tailored capacity plan, then team selection and ramping.
- Buyers compare against onshore headcount cost and against other offshore providers; the credibility question is always "will they actually understand strata?"
- Peer validation matters enormously: the site leads with named, real testimonials from agency principals and from officeholders of industry bodies (SCA Australasia, SCA South Australia, SCA NSW, CCI British Columbia) plus the firm's own memberships of those bodies.
- Volume is the proof of operational maturity: 200,000+ individual strata tasks handled per month, 500+ placements, 98% retention, live since April 2019.
- Work is structured per role, and each role has a documented training module list (5–8 modules per role).
- The hiring side runs a real pipeline: named open positions with department, work site (Angeles City / San Fernando City), shift, employment type, responsibilities, qualifications and benefits.
- Dual geography: Australia (Ivanhoe, Victoria) and Canada (Vancouver, BC), with a Canada-localised home page variant.
- Delivery offices in the Philippines (Mabalacat, Angeles City, Marisol); the company history — three computers and a water dispenser in 2019, through earthquakes, lockdowns and COVID — is genuine and is used as brand material.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Every claim carries a real number, a named person, a named platform, or a membership logo. Never soften a real statistic into a vague adjective.
2. **Strata literacy is the product.** The site must read as written by people who know what a levy query, a Section 184 and an AFSS actually are. Generic BPO language is a positioning failure.
3. **The discovery call is the only conversion.** Every page has one obvious next step and it is the free 30-minute call; the capacity test and job applications are the only secondary actions.
4. **Dual-geography by default.** Australia and Canada are peers, not a primary and an afterthought.
5. **Two-sided honesty.** The agency buyer and the Philippines-based professional are both real audiences; the careers side must be as credible as the sales side.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Web content must meet WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard-operable navigation including the mobile menu and tabs, visible focus states, colour contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, semantic landmarks and heading order, accessible names on all icon-only controls, and full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Target audiences are largely 35–60 and often reading on a laptop between meetings.
