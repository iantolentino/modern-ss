How we work this out

Managers × hours a day × 5 days × 48 working weeks (four weeks are left out for leave and public holidays). One full-time specialist works about 1,920 hours a year, so the team size is the hours divided by 1,920.

What if your managers got those hours back?

The goal isn't simply to add cheaper staff. It's to move the right work to the right level of resource, and free your experienced people to do the work only they can do.
