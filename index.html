FAQ

Common questions

Anything else? Ask our solutions team.

Where is the Strata Staff team based?

Our team works from our Marisol Office in Angeles City, Pampanga, Philippines. Strata Staff started in a small office in Mabalacat, Pampanga in April 2019. Client offices are in Ivanhoe, Victoria and Vancouver, British Columbia.

Are your staff trained in strata?

Yes. Our staff are trained specifically in strata operations and are familiar with Australian and Canadian strata legislation. New staff go through our Foundation Training Program, and every role has its own course outline through the Strata Staff Academy.

Which strata software can your team use?

Our staff are familiar with 13+ platforms, including StrataMax, Urbanise, Stratafy, MRI, Strata Vault, PIQ, Smata, MR5 and Buildium.

How do we get started?

Book a free 30-minute discovery call. We design an offshore capacity plan around your roles and platforms, then select, vet and train your staff before they join your workflows.

Can you cover different time zones?

Yes. We provide 24/7 offshore capacity for our client partners in Australia and Canada, including permanent nightshift teams.

Can we visit our offshore team?

Yes. Our Marisol Office has a client lounge and meeting rooms, and our team can arrange accommodation, transport and a company vehicle for your visit.
