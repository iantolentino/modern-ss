Announcement15 July 2025

Strata Staff Joins SCA South Australia

Strata Staff is now a corporate member of the Strata Community Association South Australia (SCA SA).

We're proud to announce that Strata Staff is now a corporate member of the Strata Community Association South Australia (SCA SA).

This is another step in our commitment to the strata industry. As a company delivering offshore staffing solutions for strata and property professionals, we believe industry alignment is key to long-term success, for our clients and the communities they serve.

With membership in both the New South Wales and South Australia chapters of the Strata Community Association, we are:

  • Deepening our support for strata businesses across regions
  • Reinforcing our dedication to quality, trust and collaboration
  • Strengthening our connection to the evolving needs of the strata sector

We look forward to contributing to the SCA community.
