Human Resources Assistant

Administrative and operational HR support with a focus on industrial relations, HR compliance and employee relations.

Angeles City, Pampanga Full-time

The HR Assistant provides administrative and operational support across HR functions, with a primary focus on Industrial Relations, HR Compliance and Employee Relations. The role assists with employee relations cases, disciplinary matters, HR documentation, investigations and compliance, and supports employee engagement, recruitment, onboarding, records, attendance and leave administration, and performance management.

Responsibilities

Industrial and employee relations

  • Assist in managing and monitoring employee relations and industrial relations cases
  • Prepare and process Notices to Explain (NTEs), written warnings, disciplinary notices and other HR correspondence
  • Coordinate administrative hearings, employee meetings and case discussions
  • Gather and organise documentation required for investigations
  • Maintain accurate and confidential records of employee relations and disciplinary cases

HR compliance

  • Maintain HR compliance records and documentation
  • Prepare documents for internal audits, compliance reviews and government requirements
  • Track compliance-related activities and deadlines

General HR administration

  • Prepare HR reports, trackers, presentations and documentation
  • Prepare HR-related letters, certificates and notices
  • Respond to employee enquiries and requests

Recruitment, onboarding and engagement

  • Support the recruitment team with coordination activities
  • Support orientation and onboarding
  • Help plan employee engagement activities, HR events and surveys

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Psychology
  • 0–1 year of HR experience, particularly in industrial relations
  • Knowledge of labour standards and due process
  • Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
  • Discretion and confidentiality with sensitive records
  • Clear written and verbal communication

Benefits

  • Health insurance
  • Life insurance
  • Paid training
  • Birthday leave
  • Company events
  • Opportunities for career growth
