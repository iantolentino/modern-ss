Accounting

Junior Accountant

Process invoices, levies, owner charges and reimbursements for strata clients. Fresh graduates are encouraged to apply.

Angeles City, Pampanga Full-time Nightshift (Permanent)

The Junior Accountant provides accounting and administrative support to client operations by processing financial transactions, maintaining accurate accounting records, and preparing financial documentation. The role ensures the timely and accurate processing of invoices, owner charges, reimbursements, levy administration and financial records.

This position suits people who are detail-oriented, analytical and eager to build a career in accounting. Comprehensive training is provided.

Responsibilities

Financial transaction processing

  • Process invoices and financial documents accurately and on time
  • Assist in processing Funds on Behalf (FOB) transactions
  • Process owner reimbursements and verify supporting documentation
  • Assist in processing lot owner charges

Strata levy administration

  • Prepare and process strata levy transactions
  • Generate and distribute levy notices to property owners
  • Maintain accurate levy records

Administrative support

  • Create and update supplier records
  • Process work orders and quotation requests
  • Generate strata certificates and other finance documents

Data management

  • Enter and maintain accurate financial information
  • Review and validate supporting documents before processing
  • Assist with reconciliation and data verification

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Accountancy, Accounting Technology, Management Accounting, Financial Management or a related course
  • Fresh graduates are highly encouraged to apply
  • Basic understanding of accounting principles
  • Proficient in Microsoft Excel
  • Strong numerical aptitude and attention to detail
  • Good written and verbal English

Benefits

  • Health insurance
  • Life insurance
  • Paid training
  • Company events
  • Opportunities for promotion
