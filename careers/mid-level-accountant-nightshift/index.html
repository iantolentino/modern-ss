Accounting

Mid-Level Accountant (Nightshift)

Manage accounts payable, reconciliations, levies, budgets and year-end preparation for strata-titled properties.

Angeles City, Pampanga Full-time Nightshift (Permanent)

The Mid-Level Accountant manages mid-tier financial operations for strata-titled properties. Positioned between junior and senior roles, this position prepares and reviews financial statements, executes accounts payable and bank reconciliations, drafts property budgets, manages levy operations, and handles end-of-year financial preparation.

Responsibilities

Accounts payable and banking

  • Oversee invoice and credit processing and execute payment runs
  • Process lot owner charges and reimbursements
  • Perform daily bank downloads and reconciliations

Levy management

  • Strike levies, issue levy notices and handle debt recovery
  • Respond to levy enquiries from owners and clients by email and phone

Budgeting and financial tracking

  • Draft property budgets and reconcile accounts

Financial year-end

  • Assist in year-end closing, including adjusting charges and interest, reallocating codes and funds, and preparing files for audit

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Accountancy, Accounting Technology, Finance or a related course
  • 2–4 years of experience in general accounting, accounts payable or financial reporting
  • BPO, real estate or offshore client experience is highly preferred
  • Strong end-to-end accounting skills (AP, AR, bank reconciliations, GL)
  • Intermediate to advanced Excel
  • Clear written and verbal English for levy queries

Benefits

  • Health insurance
  • Life insurance
  • Paid training
  • Birthday leave
  • Company events
  • Opportunities for career growth
