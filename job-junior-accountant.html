Item 03

About the job

Processes financial transactions and maintains accounting records under client procedures and company standards.

Ideal for detail-oriented, analytical candidates. Training provided.

Item 04

Responsibilities, requirements and benefits

Responsibilities

Financial Transaction Processing

  • Process invoices and financial documents accurately and on time.
  • Process Funds on Behalf (FOB) transactions.
  • Process owner reimbursements; verify supporting documents.
  • Process lot owner charges and other transactions.
  • Maintain accurate accounting records and documentation.

Strata Levy Administration

  • Process strata levy transactions in the system.
  • Issue levy notices to owners.
  • Maintain levy records and documentation.

Administrative Support

  • Create and update supplier records.
  • Process work orders and quotation requests.
  • Generate strata certificates and finance documents.
  • Perform filing, document management and electronic record keeping.
  • Maintain financial files, trackers and records.

Data Management

  • Enter and maintain financial data in company systems.
  • Validate supporting documents before processing.
  • Reconcile records and verify data.
  • Keep financial records accurate and current.

Team Support

  • Collaborate with teams to meet accounting deadlines.
  • Assist with accounting reports and administrative projects.
  • Perform other accounting and administrative duties as assigned.

Requirements

Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor's Degree in Accountancy, Accounting Technology, Management Accounting, Financial Management or related business courses.
  • Fresh graduates encouraged to apply.

Experience

  • Accounting, bookkeeping, finance, administrative or data entry experience is an advantage, not required.
  • Accounting or finance internship or OJT is an advantage.

Technical Skills

  • Basic understanding of accounting principles and processes.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Good computer navigation and data entry skills.
  • Ability to learn accounting software and client systems quickly.
  • Strong numerical aptitude and attention to detail.

Soft Skills

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • High accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Excellent organisational and time management skills.
  • Good written and verbal English communication.
  • Ability to prioritise tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Ability to work independently and within a team.
  • Professional and dependable; maintains confidentiality and integrity with financial information.
