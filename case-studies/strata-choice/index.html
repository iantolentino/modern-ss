How Strata Choice grew its offshore team from 5 to 21

Strata Choice started with 5 Strata Staff team members in 2023. Today 21 strata-trained specialists support its managers.

5 → 21team members since 2023
4.2×growth of the offshore team

The challenge

Strata Choice is a major player in the strata industry. As it kept growing, it needed a partner that could help with its business cost challenges without slowing the business down.

What we built

Strata Staff became Strata Choice's offshore strategic partner in 2023, starting with a team of 5. Rather than a fixed handover, the two businesses built the offshore team together, adding people as Strata Choice's requirements grew. Today the team is 21 strong.

Results in the client’s words

We have developed a strong sense that we have a partner who not only assists us with our business cost challenges but also collaborates with us in building our offshore team. We are grateful that Strata Staff became our offshore strategic partner in 2023. We started with them with only 5 staff and now we have grown to 21 headcounts.
