Strata Staff Joins the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) British Columbia Chapter
By Strata StaffNovember 7, 2025August 3, 2026Announcement
Strata Staff is proud to be a new member of the Canadian Condominium Institute (CCI) British Columbia Chapter.
This membership represents more than just an expansion of our network. It reflects our ongoing commitment to supporting condominium and strata management professionals across Canada through reliable offshore solutions.
By joining CCI British Columbia, we gain opportunities to connect with industry leaders, exchange knowledge, and collaborate on innovative ways to improve operational efficiency, reduce workload pressure, and enhance client service for condominium and strata management teams.
For Strata Staff, this membership strengthens our role as a trusted offshoring business solutions partner that understands the unique needs of the Canadian condominium and strata industry, helping management companies focus on their core operations while we take care of the rest.
