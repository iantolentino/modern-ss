Strata Staff Unveils Its Upgraded Marisol Office
By Strata StaffJune 25, 2025June 25, 2025Announcement
We’ve Upgraded Our Space.
We have made some exciting upgrades to our Marisol Office to create a more comfortable and productive environment for both our team members and visiting clients.
Whether you're dropping by for a quick check-in or spending the day with your offshore team, we've designed our space to make your visit as smooth, convenient, and enjoyable as possible.
Here's What's New for Visiting Clients:
Full Concierge Support
From arranging accommodation to transport bookings, our team is here to help. For added convenience, we also offer a dedicated company vehicle for local travel.
Exclusive Access to Our New Client Lounge
Enjoy our newly built client lounge, designed for comfort, focused work, and productive meetings.
Meeting Room Access
Whether you're holding team training sessions, coaching discussions, or 1-on-1s, our meeting rooms are available for your use.
Safe and Secure Environment
Our offices are equipped with 24/7 building security and a secure key-door lock system to ensure peace of mind.
Planning a visit?
For enquiries or to schedule your visit, feel free to contact Patty at patty@stratastaffglobal.com
We look forward to welcoming you to our new space.
