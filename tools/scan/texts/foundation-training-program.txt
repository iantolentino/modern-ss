Foundation Training Program
Strata Staff’s Learning Delivery Team offers comprehensive foundational training programs.
Excellence with Our Foundation Training Program
The Foundation Training Program (FTP) aims to train aspiring strata learners in basic foundation skills, which will help them be better prepared upon endorsement to a strata agency partner. This program will help increase the “industry readiness” of professionals who want to start a career in the Strata Industry
