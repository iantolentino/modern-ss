Strata Staff Joins SCA South Australia
By Strata StaffJuly 15, 2025July 15, 2025Announcement
We’re proud to announce that Strata Staff is now a corporate member of the Strata Community Association South Australia (SCA SA).
This marks another meaningful step in our ongoing commitment to the strata industry. As a company dedicated to delivering offshore staffing solutions tailored for strata and property professionals, we believe that industry alignment is key to long-term success — for both our clients and the communities they serve.
With membership in both the New South Wales and South Australia chapters of the Strata Community Association, we are:
Deepening our support for strata businesses across regions
Reinforcing our dedication to quality, trust, and collaboration
Strengthening our connection to the evolving needs of the strata sector
Our team remains focused on helping strata businesses scale efficiently and sustainably through reliable offshore support that reflects local standards, best practices, and client expectations.
We look forward to contributing to the SCA community and continuing to be a trusted partner for the growing needs of the strata sector.
