Mid-Level Accountant (Nightshift)
📍 Nightshift (Permanent) · Angeles City
Position Details
Department:Operations
Reports To:Team Leader / Client Point-of-Contact
Employment Type:Full-Time (Permanent)
Work Site:Main Office: Angeles City
Work Schedule:Monday to Friday (09:00PM to 06:00AM)
About the Job
The Mid-Level Accountant manages mid-tier financial operations for strata-titled properties. Positioned between junior and senior roles, this position prepares and reviews financial statements, executes accounts payable and bank reconciliations, drafts property budgets, manages levy operations, and handles end-of-year financial preparations.
Responsibilities
Accounts Payable & Banking Management
Oversee invoice and credit processing, execute payment runs, process lot owner charges/reimbursements, and perform daily bank downloads and reconciliations.
Levy Management & Communication
Strike levies in the system, issue levy notices, handle debt recovery processes, and respond to email/phone inquiries regarding levies from owners and clients.
Budgeting & Financial Tracking
Draft property budgets, log management activities, and reconcile accounts to ensure transparent fund allocation for operations.
Financial Year-End Preparation:
Assist in year-end financial closing, including adjusting charges and interest, reallocating codes and funds, and preparing files for audit compliance.
Requirements
Qualifications:
A. Education
• Bachelor’s Degree in Accountancy, Accounting Technology, Finance, or related business courses (Open to fresh graduates).
B. Experience
• Minimum of 2–4 years of experience in general accounting, accounts payable, or financial reporting (prior experience in BPO, real estate, or offshore client operations is highly preferred).
C. Technical Skills
• Strong proficiency in end-to-end accounting processes (AP, AR, Bank Reconciliations, GL).
• Intermediate to advanced MS Excel skills (data analysis, formulas, financial formatting).
• Aptitude for navigating specialized strata accounting platforms and ERP tools.
D. Soft Skills
• High analytical skills and strong numerical accuracy.
• Excellent written and verbal English communication skills for handling levy queries.
• Strong time management and problem-solving abilities
