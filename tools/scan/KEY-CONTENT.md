Enjoying The Journey
Guided by the utmost aim of helping strata agencies with specialized capacity solutions, Strata Staff embarked its humble beginnings in a small office in Mabalacat Pampanga way back 2019.
Humble Beginnings
Trevor McGuinness (Owner/Co-Founder) and Paul Miller (Owner/Co-Founder) were deeply interested in a possible business concept in the Philippines. They discussed this as a group of entrepreneurs, amongst other ventures they wanted to try out. After much deliberation and consultation, plus several trips to the Philippines, Paul and Trevor decided to start beta testing Strata administration work in the Philippines.
Strata Staff was launched in April 2019, seeing valuable capabilities in servicing Strata companies with high-end business admin work offshore. By late 2020, Dan Fabros joined Strata Staff, helping Paul and Trevor with operations and business scalability.
Starting with three computers and a water dispenser, Strata Staff dedicated countless hours to documenting every strata admin task, even while servicing some agencies with just casual hours during that time. Despite facing roadblocks such as earthquakes, lockdowns, and the COVID-19 Pandemic, Strata Staff remained steadfast and continued to push forward, demonstrating resilience and a commitment to meeting challenges head-on.
Since the launch in April 2019, Strata Staff has enjoyed rapid growth in terms of the number of clients and the volume of strata administrative tasks routinely processed offshore daily. From processing the basic strata administrative tasks like archiving to complicated property and strata administration tasks like S184/S22 to handling levy queries and preparation of financial bank statements to inbox management and drafting AGM agendas – Strata Staff is currently handling as many as 200,000+ individual strata tasks per month.
Core Values
At Strata Staff, we have an inclusive and intentional work environment guided by our values and culture. We continually aim to improve our service levels to strengthen strategic partnerships. Our people define and echo the quality of our engagements, which has been substantiated by awesome client testimonials. We welcome diversity and adapt to the ever-changing and dynamic requirements of strata agencies.

Humble Journey
Guided by the utmost aim of helping strata agencies with specialized capacity solutions, Strata Staff embarked it’s humble beginnings in a small office in Mabalacat Pampanga way back 2019.
How It All Began
Trevor McGuinness (Owner/Co-Founder) made some initial trips to the Philippines in 2015-2016 thinking of a possible business concept that can provide employment opportunities. Upon Trevor’s return to Australia, he discussed the offshoring business model with his friend Paul Miller (Owner/Co-Founder). Having shared values and vision for the concept, Trevor and Paul decided they would beta test Strata administration work in the Philippines. Seeing the potential for a specialist BPO helping STRATA companies with high-end business admin work offshore, our Co-Founders hard launched Strata Staff in April 2019.
Starting with three computers and a water dispenser did not prevent Strata Staff in dedicating hours after hours documenting every strata admin tasks whilst servicing some agencies with just casual hours during that time. Roadblocks like earthquake, lockdowns and the COVID 19 Pandemic did not deter Strata Staff in pushing forward responding to the challenge despite the adversity.
Since our launch in April 2019, Strata Staff has enjoyed rapid growth, both in terms of the number of clients we service, but also the volume of Strata administrative tasks we routinely process off-shore, each and every day. From processing basic strata administrative tasks like archiving to complicated property and strata administration tasks like S184/S22 to handling levy queries and preparation of financial bank statements to inbox management and drafting AGM agenda – Strata Staff is currently handling as many as 100,000+ individual business tasks per month.
At Strata Staff we have an inclusive and intentional work environment as guided by our values and culture. We continually aim to improve our service levels to ensure that strategic partnerships are strengthened. Our people define and echo the quality of our engagements which has been substantiated by awesome client testimonials. We welcome diversity and adapt to the ever-changing and dynamic requirements of strata agencies.

About the Job
The Junior Accountant provides accounting and administrative support to the client's operations by processing financial transactions, maintaining accurate accounting records, preparing financial documentation, and assisting with day-to-day accounting activities. The role ensures the timely and accurate processing of invoices, owner charges, reimbursements, levy administration, and financial records while maintaining compliance with established client procedures and company standards.
This position is ideal for individuals who are detail-oriented, analytical, and eager to build a career in accounting. Comprehensive training will be provided to equip successful candidates with the knowledge and skills required to support client operations effectively.
Responsibilities
Financial Transaction Processing
Process invoices and financial documents accurately and in a timely manner.
Assist in processing Funds on Behalf (FOB) transactions.
Process owner reimbursements and verify supporting documentation.
Assist in processing lot owner charges and other financial transactions.
Maintain accurate accounting records and financial documentation.
Strata Levy Administration
Prepare and process strata levy transactions within the accounting system.
Generate and distribute levy notices to property owners.
Maintain accurate levy records and supporting documentation.
Administrative Support
Create and update supplier records within the accounting system.
Process work orders and quotation requests.
Generate strata certificates and other finance-related documents.
Perform filing, document management, and electronic record keeping.
Maintain organized financial files, trackers, and operational records.
Data Management
Enter and maintain accurate financial information within company systems.
Review and validate supporting documents before processing transactions.
Assist with record reconciliation and data verification activities.
Ensure financial records are complete, accurate, and up to date.
Team Support
Collaborate with internal teams to ensure timely completion of accounting processes.
Assist with accounting reports and administrative projects as required.
Perform other accounting and administrative duties assigned in support of client operations.

Foundation Training Program
Strata Staff’s Learning Delivery Team offers comprehensive foundational training programs.
COURSE OBJECTIVES
The Strata Property Management 101 training programme will provide fundamental knowledge on Strata Properties, Strata Management Processes and the Roles & Responsibilities of the vested parties. The programme comprises the following six modular courses to facilitate learners to choose a course/s which they have a direct vested interest in and the flexibility to choose other course/s which they may wish to know more about.
Each course is of 3 hours learning duration with Q&A followed by a networking session for participants to share, learn and explore collaboration for the betterment of the strata management industry. The courses will be conducted in either face-to-face mode or via on-line live streaming.

COURSE LEADERS
Dr Quah Lee Kiang PhD, MSc (Maintenance Management), BSc Building, FRICS
Dr Quah is the Director of the Real Estate & Construction Centre and the Real Estate & Construction Academy in Singapore. She has extensive regional and international experience in Project and Facilities Management & Maintenance research, advice and academia. Dr Quah led the EU funded project BE4213 Condition Assessment and Maintenance Strategies for Buildings and Building Components, which among other things, sought to develop an objective building condition assessment system. This project formed part of ongoing research over the last 20 years which culminated in Dutch Standard NEN 2767 on Condition Assessment of Buildings, believed to be a ‘world first’.  Dr Quah’s professional and research affiliations include being Coordinator of the International Council for Research and Innovation in Building and Construction (CIB) Working Commission 70 on Facilities Management and Maintenance for a decade and past President of the Chartered Institute of Building Singapore Centre. She was also a member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) Asia Board, Singapore Board and Asia Pacific Sustainability Board. Dr Quah was conferred an Honorary Fellow by Oxford Brookes University for her work in real estate and construction education and the unusual impact her career has had on this particular academic field in Asia.
Michael Tan Kie Thye
Michael Tan has over 35 years of experience in property management handling mainly strata-titled residential and commercial properties. His expertise is in the application of strata laws in operations and is well-versed with two-tier management corporations. Michael had held senior appointments with several large international property consultants such as Jones Lang LaSalle, Colliers International, Savills CKH Property Management and Knight Frank Singapore. Michael is a certified adult trainer and lectures on Strata Management in the Real Estate & Construction Academy. He also provides consultancy advice to a number of Management Corporations on matters ranging from the appointment of MAs and BMSMA compliance matters.
